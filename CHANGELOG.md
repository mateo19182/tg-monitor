# Change log

## 2026-10-12

- Links archive (`links.py`, `links.db`): 2.96M URL mentions from 1.37M messages indexed by canonical URL, domain and kind; t.me references (1.1M) kept as a discovery feed of channels referenced but not watched; shortener resolution and title fetching run on demand. Story pages show provenance: the first channel to post and the order in which tiers picked the story up.
- Watchlist trimmed from 471 to 297 channels to keep the focus on politics and disinformation: mainstream media except eight reference outlets, institutions, unions and civil-society groups, diaspora community channels, provincial party branches, regional media, Latin American embassies and local channels moved to `channels_cut.csv` with a reason each. None of the removed channels had been archived yet.
- Stories on the site: `#/stories` (week, region and theme filters), `#/s/<id>` (summary, chain across weeks, channels, member posts with Telegram links) and `#/threads` (story chains of at least N weeks with per-week bars). EN/ES titles and summaries. Weeks 33–41 labelled (about 240 stories per week, a quarter of posts unassigned); labelling runs through the Batches API with thinking disabled, $0.0013 per story.
- Arenas restructured into two axes, region and theme, so that labels do not overlap (e.g. "Ukraine war" = region russia_ukraine + theme war_defence). Added justice, housing and social, media and technology, protests, Maghreb, Africa and Asia-Pacific. Model proposals for new themes are stored in `arena_proposals` with a definition and the closest listed theme (`stories.py --proposals`). Comments are excluded from embedding and stories by default. `sync.sh` embeds new texts after each hourly run; `stories.sh` clusters and labels the previous week every Monday (cron).
- Added the story layer: `embed.py` (bge-m3 vectors in `vec.db`, texts normalised to drop URLs, handles-only lines and share footers), `stories.py` (weekly UMAP+HDBSCAN grouping, cross-week chaining at cosine ≥ 0.90 with a related band at 0.85, labelling through the Message Batches API with a cached system prompt and a spend ledger) and `arenas.csv` (17 arenas, EN/ES). Chosen after a benchmark on 19k posts of week 41: bge-m3 and harrier-0.6b gave the same structure (≈250 groups, 37% noise), bge-m3 2.6× faster. First labelled sample: weeks 40–41 posts and week 41 comments; 0.13 cents per story in batch mode.
- Rewrote `SUBMISSION_DRAFT.md` around a broad archive of political communication on Telegram relevant to Spain, with classification, clustering and analysis over time. Kept labels, models and the observation window provisional; topic similarity versus sharing is now an optional direction. The LOI distinguishes the ambition of wide coverage from the current purposive sample and incomplete collection. Nothing has been submitted.
- Added 28 verified public channels from Maldita.es' December 2022 and July 2025 samples, bringing the watchlist to 219. Added the December 2022 source as `M13` and a neutral category for public author, association and community channels. Initial history starts in January 2020 for the 2022 sample and November 2024 for NOTIKAOS.
- Added `maldita-coverage.csv`, covering all 64 source-list entries. Six entries remain unresolved, unavailable or pending private-channel approval; the existing Expreso channel is a probable renamed match. Excluded unrelated or reused handle candidates. Sent a join request for Pollo NEWS; collection remains pending approval.
- Completed the Takeout benchmark on Rybar ES and InfoDefenseESPAÑOL: two rounds of 1,000 messages per channel and mode, alternating mode order. Normal reads used one-second page pacing and took 37.870 seconds for 4,000 messages; Takeout used no added page delay and took 14.555 seconds, a 2.60-fold throughput increase for these configurations. Message-id hashes matched in every paired read; no flood waits occurred. Timings include history reads and row conversion, without archive writes or media downloads. This does not establish a sustained server-side quota or isolate mode from pacing. Telegram initially returned a 24-hour initialization delay, but the next attempt succeeded. Production collection still uses normal reads.
- Created `STATE_OF_THE_ART.md` and `SUBMISSION_DRAFT.md` for the archive and clustering research. The submission draft targets the letter-of-inquiry format for Kevin Munger's AI peer-review experiment; it distinguishes proposed analyses from results.

Checked benchmark sample anchoring, alternating order, initialization-delay and flood-wait outcomes, and restoration of pacing settings using simulated clients. Python compilation, shell syntax and diff whitespace checks passed. Rebuilt the site with all 219 watchlist entries and resumed collection through `sync.sh`.
